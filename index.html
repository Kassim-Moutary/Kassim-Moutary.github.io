About

Who I am

I'm Kassim Moutary, a rising senior applying to engineering and computer engineering programs this fall. What draws me to this field is the ability to understand how things actually function — and to solve real problems by building something rather than just reading about it.

That curiosity has taken a few different shapes so far: training a computer vision model to recognize faces during a week-long AI camp at VCU, working through robotics challenges with a team at RITS Robotics Academy, and now starting to build embedded systems projects of my own with Arduino — learning circuits, sensors, and code from the ground up.

This site tracks that progress as it happens — a working portfolio, not a finished one.
